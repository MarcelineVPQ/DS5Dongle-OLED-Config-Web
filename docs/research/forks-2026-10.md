# DS5Dongle forks: what they have, what they fixed, and what's worth taking

*Report for the OLED Edition (firmware `~/projects/DS5Dongle`, app `~/projects/DS5Dongle-OLED-Config-Web`), 2026-10-12. All research was read-only, and every "we have this bug" below comes from reading our code. None of it has been reproduced on hardware unless stated.*

## Summary

- **We are still the most complete PC product.** No fork has an OLED, 4 pairing slots, in-dongle calibration and stick curves, Edge Fn/back remapping, a narrator, per-stage timing diagnostics, a cross-platform app with per-game profiles, or a signed update feed with a Development channel. We also keep the gamepad descriptor byte-identical (289 bytes). Forks that add personas, a wake keyboard or a mouse interface break native DualSense games in some cases, and artzox's README admits it.
- **Reading other forks' fixes turned up about 12 real bugs in our code.** Most are small:
  - Mix-mode haptics come out 6 dB quiet.
  - Steam rumble on Windows is probably dropped.
  - Any authentication hiccup deletes the pairing.
  - A speaker FIFO race can click.
  - "Save FAIL" counts as saved.
  - Uptime wraps every 71.6 min.
  - The host can blank the lightbar the dongle owns.
  - Plus a few more, listed in section 4.
- **Every active fork runs at the stock 150 MHz.** They manage it by moving the core0 Bluetooth/USB hot path into RAM. We run at 320 MHz / 1.20 V. Their builds suggest our overclock covers for flash (XIP) misses on core0, not a CYW43 limit. This is unverified on our build, and changing it is your call.
- **The richest fork for features is artzox/DS5Dongle-Studio** (MIT): gyro aiming, macros, a deep trigger engine, better auto-haptics and hardened USB wake. **The best fork for robustness lessons is djanice1980/DS5_Bridge** (AGPL, ideas only).
- **Licence check needed on our side:** upstream put `src/audio.cpp` under MPL-2.0 on 2026-06-06 (dbf77e3). We ported later upstream audio code, but our file has no MPL header.

### 4c. Feature designs worth borrowing (backlog candidates only)

- **Trigger Lab**:
  - Use artzox's `ds5dongle-custom-effect` v2 JSON, with its 42 real-game captures (MIT, credit artzox).
  - Use its sequencer rules: duplicate start zones flip A/B into constant clicking, bow must be positional, force is 3-bit, and a while-held effect stays latched.
  - Use its force-vs-travel visualizer as the UI model.
  - djanice shows the raw bytes and native ranges.
- **Host-side auto-haptics routing.** Linux: loteran's pw-loopback to AUX2/AUX3 plus a self-heal watchdog (MIT; Linux-only). Windows: WASAPI loopback (Windows-only). This is the product gap that matters most for the auto-haptics we already ship.
- **Converted rumble on the actuators**: heavy motor at 60 Hz, light at 160 Hz, up to 200%. For rumble-only games and Proton (artzox, MIT).
- **Player LEDs as a battery gauge** and a staged lightbar battery alert (artzox), or a battery-tier lightbar mode (loteran). Either also fixes the "Pairing slot tile shows —" row.
